How to use this guide

Each day covers one walkable neighbourhood. The four main picks (a mix of beloved classics and trendy spots) all sit within a few minutes of each other, so you can graze from lunch to dinner without crossing town. Under each day you'll also find four more good tables nearby. Every card has a photo, a short note, the address, a Website link and a Map link.
