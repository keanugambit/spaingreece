The Trip

Athens to the islands

Greece mixes ancient history with bright-blue islands. This 7-day trip starts in Athens — the old capital with its ancient ruins — then sails to two famous islands: Santorini for sunsets and Mykonos for beaches and fun. Ferries and short flights link them.

Book island hotels and ferries early for summer. Sunset dinners in Santorini can sell out weeks ahead.
