How to use this guide

Greece mixes ancient history with island life. This trip gives Athens three days — the Acropolis, the museums, and a day trip to the sea or the mountains — then sails to two famous islands: Santorini for its cliffs and sunset, and Mykonos for its white town and beaches. Below you'll find the day-by-day plan, the cities and islands, the must-see sights, the food to try, and hand-picked restaurants with a Website and Map link on each.

Tip: book island hotels and ferries early for summer — sunset dinners in Santorini can sell out weeks ahead.
